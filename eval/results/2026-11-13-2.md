## Observations

- **Exact terms are where keyword search helps.** On the five exact-term questions, the step 1 baseline (vector) ranked 65% correct reports in its top results, against 90% for keyword search and 85% for hybrid. For "pump error 15", vector search's first correct report was 7th; keyword and hybrid put one first.
- **Overall, hybrid edges ahead of the baseline:** a correct report found for 19 of 20 questions against 18 of 20, precision 61% against 53%, and MRR 0.84 against 0.77. With 20 questions these differences are suggestive, not conclusive.
- **Keyword search alone is weak on reworded questions** (MRR 0.58 against 0.80 for vector), which is why it is combined with vector search rather than replacing it.
- **Rewording changes results.** Q13 reworded as "found bent or kinked" defeated vector search (no correct report in the top 8), while keyword search found only correct ones.
- **No method found the water-damage reports (Q12)** near the top. This is a good target for later steps.
