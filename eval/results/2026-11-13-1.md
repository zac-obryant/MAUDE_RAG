## Observations

- On these 20 questions, hybrid search and the step 1 baseline (vector) find a correct report equally often (19 of 20). Hybrid ranks slightly more correct reports in the top 8 (74% against 71%). With 20 questions, a difference of one question is 5 points, so this is not evidence of a real improvement.
- Exact-term questions (error codes, model number) were easy for all three methods. The narratives are written from templates, so the embedding model matches the codes well. The test set needs harder exact-term questions before it can show whether keyword search helps with them.
- Keyword search alone failed on reworded questions (Q06, Q10, Q12), as expected. Vector search alone missed Q20 (pods falling off), which keyword search found.
- No method found the water-damage reports (Q12) in its top results. Hybrid ranked lower than vector alone here, because fusion pushed vector search's one correct report out of the top 8.
